# General rule edge cases

The generic WHR General rule is overridden by army-book rules where stated.

Priority used by the builder:

1. **Forced General** — an item or rule that explicitly makes its bearer the General overrides Leadership, BSB and regimental-champion exclusions.
2. **Prohibited General** — a model explicitly stated to be unable to act as General is never eligible under the normal rule.
3. **Normal General** — choose among otherwise eligible models with the highest Leadership when the army rule requires it.

## High Elves — Forenrond’s Sword

The bearer becomes the General by birthright regardless of Leadership, including a regimental champion or Battle Standard Bearer. The item is only legal in armies of at least 2,000 points.

## Explicit prohibitions checked

- High Elves: Korhil cannot be the General.
- Skaven: Assassins cannot be the General.
- Dark Elves: Assassins cannot be the General.
